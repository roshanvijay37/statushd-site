Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything happens in WhatsApp — you send a document to our number and get a video back in the same chat.

Why a document and not a video?

When you send a video, WhatsApp compresses it before it even leaves your phone. A document is delivered exactly as it is, so we get your original — every pixel.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes. Long iPhone videos, especially HDR ones, can take a little longer. You'll see ⏳ while we work and ✅ when it's done.

How does the free day work?

Send your first video and your free day starts by itself — no forms, no payment details. When it ends, we'll let you know in the chat. Reply there if you'd like to keep going with a 30-day subscription.

How many videos can I send?

Up to 5 a day, and up to 3 waiting at once. If you send more at once, we'll ask you to send the rest once the first ones are back.

What happens to my videos?

They're used only to make your Status-ready copy. Read our privacy policy or ask us to delete your data.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone with WhatsApp — Android or iPhone.
